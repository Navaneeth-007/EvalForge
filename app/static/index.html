02 / ENGINEERING DECISIONS

Fine-tuning is a hypothesis.
Evaluation is the evidence.

01

Train efficiently

4-bit NF4 weights, LoRA on Phi-3’s fused projections, and completion-only loss. Prompts and schemas are masked during training.

02

Keep the split honest

Spider’s database-separated train and validation splits. Schema metadata is joined by database ID; evaluation samples retain their provenance.

03

Inspect the failures

Compare base and adapter predictions on identical examples. Read-only execution, bounded queries, and per-example artifacts make errors reviewable.
